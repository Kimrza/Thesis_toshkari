## Deviations

- 2026-08-21T22:15:00Z — First attempt at this stage wedged its own gate: the three artifacts were generated by a shell script, so no `ARTIFACT_CREATED`/`ARTIFACT_UPDATED` event covered two of them, and `aidlc-lib.ts`'s completion precondition requires an audited native-tool write for every `produces[]` artifact after the human's summary confirmation. Re-saving them was then blocked by the `aidlc-review-freeze` hook, because a fresh READY receipt existed and an advisory review has a one-pass budget — so neither writing nor not writing could satisfy the engine. Recovered through the sanctioned reset: `aidlc-jump.ts execute --target units-generation --direction redo`, which cleared the receipt floor. The lesson is an ordering one, not a tooling one: a `produces[]` artifact must be authored with Write/Edit, and every byte of it must be final **before** the reviewer is dispatched.
- 2026-08-21T22:20:00Z — Second attempt applied the first attempt's advisory Major finding rather than only quoting it, at the human's explicit direction at the recovery choice. The advisory contract normally forbids applying reviewer suggestions before the gate; here the redo had already reset the receipt, the human chose "jump, redo, and fix the finding", and the fresh reviewer pass reviews the corrected text, so the finding is not riding on a stale receipt.

## Open questions (continued, second attempt)

- 2026-08-21T22:40:00Z — Human note at the learnings ritual, classified by them as an **Open question** and therefore parked rather than installed as a practice. Substance, to be applied to this stage's artifacts through the gate's Request Changes path: record all three carried-forward issues as explicit **unit-level blockers** rather than omitting the affected units or letting an unresolved artifact read as approved. (1) Map ADR-10's unsigned §12/§13.2 amendment to every affected owning unit and file — `foundation` for `src/data/config.py` and `tests/test_determinism.py`, `governance-guards` for `src/data/locked_test.py` — tracking approval state per unit and per file; the units stay in the DAG but their affected scope is not Ready, Approved or complete until the authorized project decision owner approves. (2) Keep the fixture unit and its dependencies, and mark the `plumbing_7day` manifest-dependent capability blocked by application-design Known defects row 12; no manifest may be invented, inferred or substituted, and the unit cannot pass its completion gate until the defect is resolved and the authoritative manifest is available and hash-verifiable. (3) Map the `three_seed_mean` signature defect to the unit owning confirmatory-prediction construction **and** to every downstream evaluation unit consuming that prediction, and the `fit_transforms` defect to `features-and-splits` with its downstream effect on training and evaluation exposed, because the contract must enforce train-only fitting and prevent validation or locked-test leakage; both are resolved as governed cross-unit contracts — input and output types, alignment requirements, fitted-state ownership, allowed partitions, failure conditions — before their affected units enter functional design or implementation. Structural existence and readiness stay distinct: a unit may be present in the DAG and carry documented dependencies and ownership while blocked, but a blocked unit is neither sequenced for implementation nor marked accepted until its named blocker is discharged. Unit metadata carries blocker ID, affected artifact, owning unit, downstream units, required resolution, approval authority and current status.
