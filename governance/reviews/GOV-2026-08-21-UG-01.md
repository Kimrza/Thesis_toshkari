### `UG-01` — the protected set's derivation from TE §7.0B is unstated, and three §7.0B immutables have no visible mapping

- **Seat / severity:** Implementation Reviewer · **MAJOR** (corrected down from BLOCKER)
- **Gate / stage:** G-P2, G-P3C, NFR-PHASE-01, TA-27 / 2.7, inherited from 2.6
- **Board correction, recorded rather than quietly amended.** The review as first issued stated that "no fourteen-item list appears in the Vision, the Technical Environment, or any stage artifact." That is wrong. `requirements.md` FR-P1-06-1 enumerates exactly fourteen items — model source; TensorFlow/Keras environment; architecture serialization; feature manifest; target contract; split/mask manifests; grids; selected hyperparameters; optimizer/loss policy; seeds; metrics; statistical configuration; bootstrap; reporting hierarchy — derived count **14**, origin `IMPL-1` (`GOV-2026-08-20-RA-01`), approved at stage 2.3. The board checked the Vision and the TE and did not grep the requirements artifact. No cardinality is in dispute and none is deferred.
- **Evidence for what remains open.** Derived against the authority: TE §2.2 line 121 lists **12** items; TE §7.0B line 333 lists **16**; FR-P1-06-1's fourteen are §2.2's twelve plus `bootstrap` and `reporting hierarchy`. FR-P1-06-1 states the set as "the union of TE §2.2 and §7.0B" but records no deduplication or subsumption rule. **`history window`**, **`station encoding`** and **`baselines`** are named immutable in §7.0B and appear in none of the fourteen.
- **Observed mismatch:** `history window` and `station encoding` are plausibly inside `feature manifest`, and `embargo` inside `split/mask manifests`, but no artifact says so. `baselines` — M-01 persistence, M-02 seasonal persistence, M-03 climatology — has no plausible home among the fourteen at all.
- **Consequence:** `diff_protected_hashes` can return the empty mapping that *is* G-P3C's pass condition while a Phase 2 confirmatory run has changed a baseline definition, a history window or a station encoding. That is protected-protocol drift passing undetected at a full-board gate.
- **Remediation applied (revised 2026-08-22 on the decision owner's reaffirmed directive).** Every cardinality claim in the 2.6 and 2.7 design artifacts is removed and replaced with "the canonical protected set derived from the union of TE §2.2 and TE §7.0B", with the **final enumeration and cardinality explicitly deferred to stage 3.1**. No artifact in either stage now states a number. An earlier remediation pass instead pointed these statements at FR-P1-06-1 as the enumeration's settled home; the decision owner reaffirmed the deferral, and that is what is now in place. FR-P1-06-1 is recorded in BLK-06 as the **approved candidate list 3.1 must validate**, not as the canonical set, and the requirement itself is **not edited** by this stage.
- **Recorded tension, deliberately unresolved.** FR-P1-06-1's acceptance criterion asserts the hash-diff key list "equal to the fourteen-item enumeration". If 3.1's canonical enumeration differs from that list in content or size, FR-P1-06-1 must be amended through Vision §15.2 before the difference is official. BLK-06 records this; no reading is adopted, and neither the requirement nor the Technical Environment is amended.
- **Closure evidence:** the canonical item-by-item enumeration; its documented deduplication rule; the cardinality calculated and printed from that enumeration; `protected_hashes.keys()` asserted to match it exactly; validation failing on a missing, extra, renamed or changed item; owner approval of the enumeration before G-P2 and G-P3C.
- **Owner / due gate:** `functional-design` (3.1), before it designs `phase_contract.py`. Enumeration approval: the authorized project decision owner under the recorded authority equivalence. Any change to FR-P1-06-1's item set or to TE §2.2/§7.0B: Vision §15.2. No governing document makes a separate supervisor signature mandatory beyond that equivalence.
