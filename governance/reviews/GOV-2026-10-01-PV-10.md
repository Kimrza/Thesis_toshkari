### Recommendation 6
**Category**: Functional. **Severity**: High (MAJOR). **Seat/ID**: ML ML-08.
**Issue**: `cross_environment_tolerance.py` excludes only NaN. An `inf` in an (a) value makes `scale`, and therefore the floor and the tolerance, infinite. The seat's probe confirmed that `check_candidate` then accepts anything. Matching infinities also give `abs(inf-inf)=nan`, which is skipped silently.
**Importance**: §A8 item 9 says the tolerance is "never widened". The integrity posture (Q12-B) says never pass a violated invariant silently.
**Risk**: One overflow disables the (c) check for a whole field.
**Possible Solutions**: (a) refuse any non-finite, non-NaN value in `freeze_tolerance` and `check_candidate`, with an `inf`-injection negative control; (b) treat `inf` like NaN, matched by position.
**Comparison**: (a) fails fast, consistent with the project's posture. (b) tolerates overflow instead of surfacing it.
**Recommendation**: (a). Owner: Student. Due: before row 39 closes.
**Decision Required**: Approve / Reject / Modify / Postpone.
