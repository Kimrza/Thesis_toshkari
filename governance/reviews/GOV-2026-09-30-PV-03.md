### High

**Recommendation 1: wrong drift count at five sites, and two drifts undisclosed** (CHAIR-01, DATA-01; Scopes A and B)
- **Sites:** the README, D-83 §R1-3, results l.30, matrix l.61, and PV-02 rulings §2. All five say "22 of 61 … e.g. `scipy` 1.17.1".
- **Correct figures:**
  - 6 packages are in neither lock (distlib, filelock, platformdirs, python-discovery, uv, virtualenv);
  - 16 are pinned in the conda lock, `scipy` among them;
  - two drifts were undisclosed: `fonttools` 4.65.0 vs 4.66.0, and `pytz` 2026.3.post1 vs 2026.4;
  - 0 of 20 conda builds match.
- **Options:**
  1. Re-derive against both locks, print the derivation, and correct all 5 sites with dated notes.
  2. Correct the README only.
- **Recommended:** 1. Owner: agent, before 4.6 approval.

**Recommendation 2: the DEC-repeat refusal is unimplementable, and would refuse the real G-06** (VAL-01, IMPL-01)
- **Why:**
  - `AccessRecord` and the receipt carry no `phase_id`, and 0 of 1,657 rows have one.
  - `05:728`, `06:1075` and `07:740` all open December under `locked_evaluation`, and `05` comes first.
  - Receipts sit under `<run_id>/`.
- **Options:**
  1. Add `phase_id` and `script_id` to `AccessRecord` and the receipt, and refuse on a prior `06`-scope record for the same phase.
  2. Resolve via the registry and globbing.
- **Recommended:** 1, with negative controls: `05`-then-`06` passes, a second `06` is refused, and another phase is admitted.

**Recommendation 3: the G-05 detection assertion fails open, has no home, and the D-text overstates it** (VAL-02, IMPL-04, CHAIR-03)
- **Why:**
  - An orphan `run_id`, left by an aborted read, is ignored.
  - Detection relies on an access log whose durability is unmeasured.
  - Eleven writers target an absent log.
  - There is no module and no machine-readable cutoff.
  - The D-text says it "detects any breach".
- **Options:**
  1. Make it fail closed on unresolved `run_id`s: key on the record's timestamp, put the cutoff in the D-number mirrored in config, scan every log, place it in `locked_test.py`, reword the D-text, and add negative controls.
  2. Keep it, and state the residuals.
- **Recommended:** 1.

**Recommendation 4: the Kaggle fallback includes DEC with no time bound** (ML-01, CHAIR-02)
- **Why:** it conflicts with the once-per-phase DEC rule and with custody membership `{a, b, c}`. The accepted risks were unstated.
