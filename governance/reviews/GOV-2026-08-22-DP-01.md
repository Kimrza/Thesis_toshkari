## 4. Reconstruction method, and its limits

For each readable finding ID:

1. Every citing site was read in full.
2. The finding statement was inferred **only** from what its remedy explicitly
   says the finding found. Where a citing site states the finding in its own
   words, that wording is quoted and attributed to the citing artifact — **never
   to the board**.
3. Where two sites frame one finding differently, both framings are recorded and
   the divergence is noted rather than reconciled into a single invented sentence.
4. No severity, no verdict, and no board reasoning is supplied. Where the board's
   view is unknown, this record says so.

**What this method cannot do:** it cannot distinguish a finding the board raised
from an adjacent point the owner or the remediating agent raised while acting on
it, because both arrive through the same remedy text. Each reconstruction below is
therefore the *substance the remedy attributes to the finding*, which is the
closest available proxy and not the same thing.
